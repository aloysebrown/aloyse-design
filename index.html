What we do

One practice, three disciplines

Adapting good taste and functional design principles to your style, not ours. Between us we bring fine art training, board-certified interior design, certified project management, and real estate consulting to every project.

Full Builds

Ground-up construction, managed end to end: site, permits, structure, and schedule held to a standard worth putting our name on.

Renovations

Additions and whole-home remodels that respect what a house already is while making it work for the way you live now.

Interiors

Board-certified interior design. Space planning, materials, and finishes are resolved with the same rigor as the framing behind them.

How a project runs

Heritage

Owned and operated by women named Aloyse

Aloyse Bick Heiss Culver McShane opened the practice in St. Louis, Missouri in 1950. It is a legacy passed through three generations of women who carry the name Aloyse, and it now builds from Chapel Hill. Seventy-five years of that means the judgment to tell you what a project really takes, and the record to back it up.
